About Us

Hi, we are Oleg and Marina Serba, two professional photo retouchers with 17 years of experience in the photo editing.

We are working with photographers and creative studios. We have done a lot of cool projects, including works for famous photographers and creative artists Matthew Haysom, Simon Ledder, James Mills, Nick Leary, Nicole McCluskey, Tom Rose, Wish The Label, some magazines including House of Fraser, GQ, Fabulous Magazine, Heart Beauty Magazine, Culture!, Koncierge, M2 Magazine etc., for one of the UK's leading hair salon groups Seanhanna and a lot of advertising campaigns amongst whom Bently, Jagermeister, Nike, Ford etc.
