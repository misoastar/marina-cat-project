About Us

Welcome to Misoa Retouching. We are Oleg and Marina Serba, a creative retouching duo with 26 years of experience in high-end post-production.

We specialize in collaborating with photographers, creative agencies, and global brands to deliver flawless visual narratives. Our portfolio includes editorial features for GQ, Fabulous Magazine, Koncierge, and M2 Magazine, alongside creative work for acclaimed photographers such as Matthew Haysom, Simon Ledder, and Nick Leary.

Beyond editorial, we have extensive experience in commercial advertising, proudly crafting campaigns for global industry leaders including Bentley, Jägermeister, Nike, Ford, and House of Fraser.

Whether it's fashion, beauty, or commercial advertising, we are always open to new creative challenges. Let's discuss your next project.
